Scripts

The stories behind the films. Full scripts, in English and Bengali, will be added here.

English · Brand filmFrom Stress to SuccessA landlord who thinks he can do it all learns when to let software help.

Ethan is a confident property owner who handles every tenant request, repair and payment himself. It works, until the calls start stacking up: a water problem, a maintenance job, an elderly tenant upset that nothing has been fixed.

In the middle of it, his manager asks for the list of tenants with outstanding payments. Ethan has to send him away. He realises that phone calls, notes, files and invoices are no longer enough.

He tries Property Control. Requests, maintenance and payments move into one place, tenants get faster service, and his manager tells him it was a great decision.

Property Control. Manage smarter. Save time.

English · Brand filmSarah's StoryA student's rental nightmare, told from the tenant's side of the same product.

Sarah graduates in Ottawa and moves to Toronto for her MBA. In six months she changes homes three times. One place has maintenance problems, another has payment issues, and her latest has a broken sink that has gone unfixed for two months.

She tells her cousin Laura, who suggests a property run by Mr. Jon, a landlord who uses Property Control. Sarah visits, likes it and moves in.

This time repairs are handled, payments are smooth and her requests are answered. With home finally sorted, she can focus on her studies.

Are you just renting a house, or choosing a smarter way to live?
